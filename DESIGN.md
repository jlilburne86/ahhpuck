# AhhPuck Design Direction

- Dark instrument surface with warm yellow signal accents and green readiness states.
- Red is reserved for an observed finding or blocked gate; never use color alone to communicate state.
- SF system typography for reading, monospaced labels for signal names and machine state.
- Large, glanceable metrics; charts show observed traces with legends and units.
- Evidence cards answer “what is missing?” and findings answer “what was observed?”
- No gradients, decorative car imagery, fake live telemetry, or marketing-only screenshot compositions.
- Responsive web layouts collapse to one column and preserve readable screenshot framing on mobile.
