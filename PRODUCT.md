# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vehicle owners, tuners and engineers working with unsupported Freedom FC-03
ECUs during bench capture, vehicle testing and evidence review.

## Product Purpose

AhhPuck collects trustworthy read-only ECU evidence, compares repeatable runs,
explains data quality and safety findings, and suggests the next measurement or
controlled check. Deterministic safety and assessment logic remains authoritative;
the assistant never writes to the ECU or presents an unverified calibration as safe.

## Brand Personality

Measured, clear, protective. The product should feel like a calm engineering
workbench: direct about uncertainty, useful under pressure and respectful of the
operator's judgement.

## Anti-references

Avoid autonomous-tuner language, unexplained AI scores, dashboard decoration that
competes with safety state, and generic chatbot surfaces detached from evidence.

## Design Principles

- Show the evidence before the explanation.
- Keep the next action small, concrete and reversible.
- Make uncertainty and missing measurements visible.
- Preserve the existing dense, dark, instrument-like interface.
- Keep the operator and deterministic safety gate in control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use the existing iOS accessibility patterns, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels,
contrast-safe states, color-independent status communication and reduced-motion
behavior. The interface should remain usable during bench work and in-car review.
