AHHPUCK · PLATFORM UI/UX PLAN · v0.1

Every page, what it shows, what it gates

20 pages in six stages, from owner setup to tuner-grade evidence. Every page answers one of the four product questions: what happened, can we trust the data, why did it happen, what's the safest next step.

Navigation model

TAB BAR — 5 ROOTS
LiveMapRunsReviewGarage
Live and Map need a connection. Runs, Review and Garage work offline on saved sessions.
FULL-SCREEN MODALS Recording · Critical alert · Approval. These take over the screen: the only exits are finishing the task or a stop control.
PERSISTENT CHROME Status strip on every page: transport · ECU identity · READ-ONLY lock · active session. Tap it to open Connection.
TWO DENSITIES Owner mode: plain-language summaries. Tuner mode: raw values, hashes, σ and frame counts. Same pages, a toggle in Garage.

Core loop

Profile→ Connect→ Preflight→ Baseline run→ Proposal→ Approve→ Candidate + repeat→ Assess→ Accept · Repeat · Rollback · Abort↺
STAGE 1

Onboarding

01 · FIRST RUNTO DESIGN

Welcome & safety boundary

Sets expectations before anything connects: read-only, no writes, a human supervises every test.

Shows
  • What the app does and refuses to do
  • Evidence tags explained (documented / observed / inferred / unverified)
Gate · Acknowledgement is stored with the operator profile.
02 · GARAGETO DESIGN

Vehicle profile (engine passport)

Stepped form: chassis → engine → ECU → fuel → ignition → mods → objective.

Shows
  • ECU label decode (e.g. Ab-1EU, Z-J01030)
  • Load strategy, injection, ignition, VVT, injectors, cams, ITBs
  • Progress ring: fields known vs unknown
Gate · Unknown is a real answer, not a blank. A field marked unknown that matters blocks Proposal (11), and the page says why.
03 · GARAGETO DESIGN

Phone mount calibration

Guided: mount the phone, hold still for 5 s, drive straight, brake gently.

Shows
  • Live orientation level and axis mapping
  • Calibration quality: high / low / none
  • GPS accuracy in metres
Gate · Dynamics data from an uncalibrated mount is tagged low-confidence everywhere it appears.
STAGE 2

Connect

04 · STATUS STRIP → SHEETTO DESIGN

Connection & identity

Shows the connection's state step by step: no cable → port found → bytes arriving → frames valid → ECU identified.

Shows
  • Transport, baud and framing (FCSS 9600 8N1)
  • ECU identity: verified / inferred / unknown
  • Frames, dropped/stale/out-of-order, last receive
  • Raw byte ticker (tuner mode)
Gate · A write lock that can't be toggled, always visible.
05 · LIVE → PRE-RUNTO DESIGN

Preflight checklist

Automatic checks first, then the operator confirms the rest by hand.

Shows
  • Auto: coolant warm, voltage, oil pressure at idle, lambda alive, frame rate
  • Manual: fuel, tyres, route clear, observer present
  • Each check: pass / fail / not measurable
Gate · You can't start Record until it passes. "Not measurable" lowers confidence but doesn't block.
STAGE 3

Drive & capture

06 · TAB: LIVEDESIGNED · 1a

Live dashboard

Engine first. A big readout for RPM and lambda, small tiles with normal-range markers for everything else.

Shows
  • RPM, load, lambda vs target, coolant, IAT, oil, fuel, battery, ignition, IDC, VVT, knock
  • Secondary swipe: speed, long/lat g, yaw
  • Telemetry health strip
Next · Rearrange tiles in tuner mode. Landscape layout for a dash mount.
07 · TAB: MAPDESIGNED · 1b

Map trace

The RPM × load grid, with a cursor on the cell the engine is in now. Switch between lambda error and coverage.

Shows
  • Error / hit-count heatmap, current-cell readout
  • Unvisited cells left blank (no claim)
  • Axis evidence tag (inferred until protocol is verified)
Next · Tap a cell to see its sample distribution. Third view: a session-vs-session diff.
08 · RUNS → NEWTO DESIGN

Run setup

Defines a test that can be repeated. A candidate pre-fills from its baseline so the conditions match.

Shows
  • Type: baseline / candidate / repeat
  • Objective, gear, RPM/load band, route/dyno/track
  • Fuel, ambient, notes
Gate · A repeat locks every condition field and flags any change.
09 · MODALTO DESIGN

Recording

Built for glancing while driving: three numbers, a band-progress bar and a big Stop button.

Shows
  • RPM, lambda error, elapsed
  • Target band coverage filling in
  • Frame health dot
Gate · No navigation and no touch targets under 64 px. Auto-locks the screen against stray taps.
10 · MODAL · ANY PAGETO DESIGN

Critical alert

A full-screen red takeover with sound and haptics. It runs on the fixed safety rules, never on AI.

Triggers
  • Knock, lean under load, overheat, low oil, low voltage
  • RPM/trigger anomaly, telemetry loss
Gate · Can't be dismissed while the condition is active. Locks Proposal until an investigation is logged.
STAGE 4

Experiment

11 · REVIEW → PROPOSETO DESIGN

Objective & proposal

Pick an objective (grip, drift, 0–100, drivability…). The optimiser suggests bounded fuel-cell changes.

Shows
  • Affected cells on the grid with ± values
  • Expected effect + increment cap
  • Readiness score with its breakdown, labelled as readiness, not "perfection"
Gate · Ignition is never proposed. Cells outside coverage can't be selected.
12 · PROPOSAL STEP 2TO DESIGN

Concerns & rollback plan

What could go wrong, how you'd know, and how to get back.

Shows
  • Concerns ranked by severity
  • Stop criteria in plain numbers
  • Last accepted state + hash
Gate · You must open each concern before you can continue.
13 · MODALTO DESIGN

Supervised approval

Six confirmations, each its own toggle. Ends with a press-and-hold to sign.

Confirms
  • Vehicle/ECU profile, backup & rollback
  • Change + expected effect, warnings
  • Supervised conditions, stop criteria
Gate · Creates an experiment record only. The copy says plainly that this doesn't write to the ECU.
STAGE 5

Analyse & decide

14 · TAB: RUNSDESIGNED · 1c

Run overlay

Baseline, candidate and repeat traces stacked, with one shared cursor.

Shows
  • Lambda vs target, RPM, ignition, knock
  • Cursor readouts per run
  • Run hashes
Next · Choose channels, pinch to zoom time, rotate to landscape for a full-width chart.
15 · TAB: REVIEWDESIGNED · 1d

Assessment

The decision comes first, with the evidence checks underneath.

Shows
  • Accept / Repeat / Rollback / Abort
  • Error, repeatability, completeness, coverage, knock, plausibility
  • Max next increment; ignition locked
Gate · The safety gate makes the decision. The operator can choose a more cautious one, never a riskier one.
16 · REVIEW → ASKTO DESIGN

Analyst explanation

Explains the assessment in plain language. Every sentence links to the check or chart behind it.

Shows
  • Why it passed or failed, likely causes, missing evidence
  • Suggested questions ("Why repeat?")
  • Source chips for each claim
Gate · Read-only next to the decision. It can't change the decision or raise the increment.
STAGE 6

Records & settings

17 · TAB: RUNS (ROOT)TO DESIGN

Learning ledger

A timeline of experiments: each one's decision, its change and its result.

Shows
  • Experiment cards with decision colour
  • Accepted-reference lineage
  • Filter by objective / decision
18 · LEDGER → DETAILTO DESIGN

Session detail & export

The full evidence bundle, ready to share with a tuner.

Shows
  • Files + SHA hashes, preflight, plan, approval, assessment
  • Operator notes
  • Export .zip via share sheet (no cloud)
19 · TAB: GARAGETO DESIGN

Garage & settings

Vehicles, safety thresholds, units and density.

Shows
  • Vehicle list → profile (02)
  • Protection thresholds, with where each value came from
  • λ/AFR, °C, bar · owner/tuner mode
Gate · Making a threshold looser needs a reason, and the change is logged.
20 · TUNER MODETO DESIGN

Raw frames inspector

For decoding the protocol: hex frames with their decoded fields alongside.

Shows
  • Sequence, timestamp, bytes
  • Byte → field mapping with evidence tag
  • Unknown bytes highlighted

UX rules across every page

Missing = uncertainEmpty states show a dot, never a zero or a green tick.
Evidence tag on every valueDocumented, observed, inferred or unverified, with one colour per tag everywhere.
Colour means state, not brandGreen = OK, amber = caution, red = stop, always with a glyph and a word. White = selection and progress. Cyan = target, violet = candidate. Evidence tags stay grey.
The cautious choice is always easyStop, Repeat and Rollback are one tap. Riskier steps need confirmations.
Glanceable while drivingPages used in the car: tap targets 64 px or larger, three numbers at most, alerts by sound and haptics.
Baseline is never editedHashes are visible on every run reference.
