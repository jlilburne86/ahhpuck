Traffic-light status, fuel-map scale, four new states
The colour system in 3a now applies to every screen below. Green, amber and red only ever mean status. Selection and progress are white. Fuel maps run blue (rich) to green (on target) to red (lean). Gauge tiles show OK, caution and stop bands.
